VP trial Day 10: Prosecution to present 1st witness for CF misuse

Published: July 29, 2026

MANILA, Philippines — After a two day break from the trial of Vice President Sara Duterte, the Senate impeachment court will begin to tackle Duterte’s alleged misuse of P612.5 million in confidential funds, or impeachment Article I, on Wednesday.

Marking the 10th trial day, the House prosecution panel invited as their first witnesses two former Land Bank branch managers who witnessed the Office of the Vice President’s bank transactions in the millions.

Possibly presented as the first witness is Violeta Constantino, a former branch manager of LBP Shaw Boulevard branch, where Duterte’s special disbursing officer (SDO), Gina Acosta, supposedly withdrew four separate times a total of P125 million from December 2022 to September 2023.
